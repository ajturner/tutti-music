Connect

Each track plays on its own channel, shown in the grid header. On macOS turn on the IAC Driver in Audio MIDI Setup; on Windows add a loopMIDI port. Choose an input to step-record from a keyboard or arm ● Rec. WebMIDI needs a Chromium browser.

No controller. Pair a Bluetooth gamepad and press any button.
