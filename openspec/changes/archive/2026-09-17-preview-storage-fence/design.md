# Design
In the inline script `previewHead()` writes (`scripts/build-pages.mjs`): the raw `key`, `clear`, `removeItem` and the `length` getter are kept from `Storage.prototype`, `own(storage)` lists the raw keys carrying the prefix, and the wrappers act on that list when `this` is `window.localStorage` and defer to the raw method otherwise. `Object.keys(localStorage)` still shows raw names, since named properties of a Storage object cannot be wrapped without replacing `window.localStorage`; reading is harmless, and the destructive call is fenced. No app version change: the app's files are untouched, and the workflow always builds with main's script. Tested in `test/pages.test.mjs` by writing a live-app key past the fence from a fresh iframe's unwrapped prototype, then calling `clear()` in the preview.
