# The loop is what is written
## Why
A new phrase is 64 rows, and a phrase you have started is mostly empty. Press Play on a bar of sketch and the bar comes round once every four bars: three of silence for one of music, and the pulse is lost before the idea can be judged. Getting a loop that feels like a loop means setting the phrase's rows first, then setting them again as the idea grows, which is the up-front work a sketch should not need. A traditional tracker stops at the first empty row of the song and goes back round with what is there; Tutti's phrase should do the same with what is written in it. Two more things stand in the way of building over a running loop: a note written while the phrase loops is not heard until Play is pressed again, and nothing on screen says where the loop will turn.
## What changes
Looping a phrase (Space, **Play phrase**, ⇧Space from the cursor, ● Rec, the pocket's Start) plays **what is written**: the loop ends at the end of the last bar in which any instrument sounds a note, loose or placed, held notes included, and turns there. A phrase with nothing in it loops whole, as before. The grid and the pocket's Phrase level dim the rows past the loop's end and mark the end row **↻**, so the loop is visible before it plays and grows as the writing does. While a phrase or a pattern loops, every edit reaches the loop when it next comes round, including one that lengthens it. **Loop what is written** in ⚙ View turns the rule off, per browser, for a phrase that needs its silence.
## Capabilities
- **Modified:** `playback` (transport, edits reach the loop), `grid` (rows past the loop end), `pocket-view` (Phrase and Pattern levels)
## Non-goals
Playing the arrangement or looping a section: those play every phrase at its full length, because the arrangement is deliberate. A change to the song: the phrase's rows stay what they are, and the file format does not change. Trimming by beats or by a count of empty rows: bars keep the loop musical. Fx, lanes or placements without notes do not count as written.
