# Help page
## Why
The in-app help had grown into eight dense paragraphs inside a footer disclosure, unreadable next to the grid and impossible to keep open while working.
## What changes
A standalone guide (`help.html`) with a table of contents, key tables and sections for every feature, linked from the footer and opening in a new tab so it can sit beside the app. The footer keeps a three-column quick reference of the most used keys.
## Capabilities
- **New:** `help`
## Non-goals
Searchable help, per-cell contextual help beyond the status line.
