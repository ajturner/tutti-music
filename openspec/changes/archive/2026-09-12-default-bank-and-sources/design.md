# Design
`banks.js`: the orchestra entry is registered from the instrument table with `default: true` in the catalogue and no built-in flag; `unloadBank` treats it like any bank; `restoreBanks` re-applies a persisted unload (`tutti.unloadedBanks.v1`) unless a song lists the bank; `newSong` and `normalizeSong` record `orchestra` in `song.banks`. `scripts/build-banks.mjs` gains an SSO source (`peastman/sso`), per-instrument licence and source fields in map.json, and lowercase note parsing; `scripts/build-guitar.mjs` unpacks the FreePats archive with bsdtar and maps zones from the SFZ keys (scientific pitch, verified by measurement). Instruments may carry both `samples` and a fallback `patch`. Synth: `ksBuffer` implements the extended Karplus-Strong model; `voiceParams` patches support `unison`, `spread`, `vibrato`, `breath`, `vowels`. Licences are listed in `banks/LICENSE.md`.
