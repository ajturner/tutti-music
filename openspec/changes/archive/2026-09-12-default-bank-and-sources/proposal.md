# Default bank and better sources
## Why
The orchestra still behaved differently from every other bank in the Sounds bar (a dashed chip, no unloading), and the synthesized choir, banjo and guitar were not convincing.
## What changes
- The orchestra is simply the bank that loads by default: it can be unloaded (instruments in use stay), hidden, and reloaded from its file; songs record it like any bank; an unloaded default stays unloaded across sessions unless a song needs it.
- Voice is now the Sonatina Symphonic Orchestra chorus (male and female "aah", CC Sampling Plus 1.0, attributed), with the formant synth kept as fallback.
- Guitar is now FreePats' Spanish Classical Guitar (CC0), 48 zones with two velocity layers.
- Banjo stays synthesized but the string model gained pluck-position filtering, brightness that decays over the note, body resonators tuned to a drum head, and a tension pitch drop; the guitar fallback patch gained a detuned second string and guitar-body resonators.
- The synth gained unison detune, delayed vibrato, breath noise and a chorus for its voices.
## Capabilities
- **Modified:** `sound-banks`
## Non-goals
Recorded banjo samples: none exist under an acceptable licence; Philharmonia's terms forbid redistribution as a sampler instrument.
