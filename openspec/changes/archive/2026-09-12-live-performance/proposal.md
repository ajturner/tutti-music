# Live performance

## Why
Auditioning an idea meant stopping, switching pattern, and starting again, and hearing one section alone meant muting everything else one by one. The M8's live mode and solo make a tracker playable.

## What changes
- Solo per track: shift-click or long-press the track name. With any solo, only soloed tracks sound.
- While a pattern loops, choosing another pattern queues it to take over when the loop ends; the status shows "next".

## Capabilities
- **Modified:** `playback` (solo, queue), `pattern-grid` (solo marker), `song-files` (pattern selector while playing)

## Non-goals
- Quantised mute; song-order live launching.
