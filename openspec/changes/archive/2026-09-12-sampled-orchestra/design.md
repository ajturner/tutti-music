# Design
`scripts/build-samples.mjs` lists VSCO folders through the GitHub API, keeps one round robin at the softest and loudest layer per note, downloads, trims to 3 to 6 s with a fade, downmixes and encodes with ffmpeg to AAC 56 kbps, estimates timpani pitches by autocorrelation (their names carry no note), and writes `samples/<id>/map.json` with zones `{ art, note, layer 0..1, file, source }`. `samples/index.json` aliases violins-2 to violins-1. VSCO octave numbers are one below scientific pitch; the script adds 12.

`src/core/sampler.js`: `pickZones(map, art, pitch, dyn, vel)` is pure. `SamplerSink` wraps the synth, shares its AudioContext and master compressor, lazy-loads and decodes maps per instrument, and keeps a per-track bus (gain × volume × expression × dynamics floor, stereo pan). Voices are buffer sources with playback-rate pitch shift, a short attack (longer for legato), an accent for marcato, and articulation-specific release. Missing samples route to the synth so sound never stops.

UI: `state.sound` ('samples' default, localStorage `tutti.sound`); `previewSink()` chooses the sink; `auditionPreview` routes auditions; `preloadSamples` runs at startup, song select, sound change, play and track add; progress shows in the status. Tests: pure zone selection under Node, every bundled map's files exist, and a browser test loads violins and confirms synth instruments fall back.
