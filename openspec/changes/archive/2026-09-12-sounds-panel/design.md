# Design
`SamplerSink` gains `settings` (tune, cents, trim, release per instrument; applied to playback rate, gain and release), `coverage(id)` (sampled articulations, fallback targets, zone count, state), `phrase(id, pitches, art)` for auditions, and `lastZone` with an `onZone` hook. The synth exposes an `AnalyserNode` on its output. `src/ui/sounds.js` renders the dialog, persists settings under `tutti.sounds.v1`, draws the output scope with a zero-crossing trigger each frame while open, and draws the last zone's buffer as a min/max envelope. Styling follows the M8's dense monospace, uppercase labels and family-coloured accents. No JSON change.
