# Sounds panel
## Why
The sampler had no face: you could not see whether an instrument had samples, which articulations were real, or fix a mis-tuned or unbalanced instrument without editing files.
## What changes
A Sounds panel opened from the output group: one row per instrument with its source (samples, synth, loading), its articulations marked sampled or falling back, an audition button, and tune, cents, trim and release fields kept in this browser; a live oscilloscope of the preview output and the waveform of the zone that last played.
## Capabilities
- **Modified:** `sampled-orchestra`
## Non-goals
Loading user sample libraries; editing zones.
