# Design
`src/core/instruments.js`: `registerInstrument`, `unregisterInstrument`, `placeholderInstrument`; every instrument has a `bank`. `src/core/banks.js`: catalogue (`banks/index.json`), `installBank` (resolves `samples` against the bank URL), `loadBank` (id from the catalogue or a URL), `unloadBank` (keeps instruments in use), `ensureSongBanks`. Songs carry `banks: [id|url]`; `addTrack` and `setTrackInstrument` add the bank. Sampler: an instrument's own `samples` folder wins over the orchestra index; kits play at recorded pitch and only on mapped notes. Synth: `voiceParams(family, art, patch)`, keys/plucked/drums voices, and a synthesized 808-style kit for kit instruments without samples. `scripts/build-banks.mjs` builds the bundled banks from VSCO and VCSL, measuring each VCSL instrument's octave offset from a sample because the library's naming varies. Schemas: song `banks`, instrument `bank`/`samples`/`kit`/`patch`, and `tutti-bank.schema.json`. Service worker caches bank files like samples. **Migration:** none; songs without `banks` get an empty list.
