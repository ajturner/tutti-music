# Bank showcases
## Why
Every built-in song was orchestral, so the jazz, folk and electronica banks had nothing to demonstrate them and no starting point for a composer.
## What changes
Three new examples, one per bank: Blue in F (a twelve-bar blues with a swing groove), Crossroads reel (a D major reel with bodhrán, banjo rolls and harp), Night drive (four-on-the-floor with CHA, RET, EXP and ARP commands at work). Each records its bank so it loads on open.
## Capabilities
- **Modified:** `song-files`
## Non-goals
More orchestral examples.
