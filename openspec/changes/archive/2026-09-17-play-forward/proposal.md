# Play goes forward in the Song view
## Why
Trying format 4, the composer set repeats on sections, pressed Play in the Song view and heard one thing go round for ever: "the sections don't automatically move forward after the number of repeats". The arrangement did play correctly, but only from **Play song** or Space. The highlighted **Play** button still looped the phrase under the cursor, as it does in the grid, and a section's **▶ loop** looped that section, and neither looks at a repeat count. On a phone there is no Space key, so the button that loops was the obvious one to tap. Nothing on screen said which time through was playing, and the view did not follow the music, so even a correct playback looked stuck once it left the screen.
## What changes
Play acts on what is on screen. In the grid it loops the open phrase, as before. In the Song view it plays the arrangement on from the cursor row: the phrase's repeats, then the section's, then the next section, and stops at the end; the button reads **Play from here**. **Play song** from the Song view starts at the top. Looping is said out loud: **Loop section** in the header and **⟳ loop** on a section's bar. While playing, the section's bar and the phrase's row show which time through they are on (2/3). With follow on, the Song view's cursor rides the playing row, so the map, the phrase selector and the scroll position stay with the music. The play position no longer reports the end of the previous phrase during the 60 ms lead-in.
## Capabilities
- **Modified:** `song-view` (playhead), `playback` (transport)
