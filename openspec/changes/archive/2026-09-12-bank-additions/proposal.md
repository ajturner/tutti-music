# Bank additions and hiding
## Why
The orchestra could not be put away like the other banks, and each bundle was missing instruments a composer reaches for: harp, tuba and voices in the orchestra; banjo, a wooden flute and a washboard in the folk group; guitar and the other saxes in the jazz combo.
## What changes
- Any loaded bank, including the built-in orchestra and banks a song uses, can be hidden: its instruments leave the pickers and the Sounds table but stay registered so tracks keep playing. Hidden state persists per browser.
- Orchestra: harp (VCSL concert harp), tuba (VSCO), and a synthesized choir "voice" through formant filters, in a new voice family.
- Folk: banjo (Karplus-Strong plucked string), Irish flute (VCSL baroque alto recorder standing in for a wooden flute), washboard (VSCO guiro, ratchet and claves as a kit); harmonica was already present.
- Jazz: jazz guitar (Karplus-Strong, warmer), alto and bass sax (the tenor samples with alto and bass ranges).
## Capabilities
- **Modified:** `sound-banks`, `instruments`
## Non-goals
Recorded choir, banjo or guitar samples; those libraries have none in the public domain.
