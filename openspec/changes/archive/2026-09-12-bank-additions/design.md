# Design
`hiddenBanks` (core) is a set of bank ids; `isHidden(instrumentId)`; the Sounds bar toggles it for built-in or in-use banks instead of unloading, persisted under `tutti.hiddenBanks.v1`; the instrument picker keeps a hidden bank's group only when the track's current instrument is in it. Synth: `ksBuffer(freq, ks)` renders a Karplus-Strong string (noise burst, one-period delay line, lowpass in the loop, decay to -60 dB) into a cached buffer; patches with `ks` use it as a one-shot voice. Patches with `formants` route the oscillator mix through parallel bandpass filters, giving the choir its "ah". The bank build script gains `shareSamples` (alto and bass sax point at the tenor folder) and an `extra` orchestra entry that builds harp and tuba folders only; the orchestra's bank.json still comes from the table. Families gain `voice`.
