# Fill and randomise
## Why
Repeating a figure down a section, loosening machine-perfect velocities and timing, and sketching a random in-key line were all manual. The M8's fill and randomise shortcuts do them in one press.
## What changes
Four selection operations on the toolbar: Fill, Rnd vel, Rnd pitch, Humanize.
## Capabilities
- **Modified:** `selection-batch`
## Non-goals
Undo granularity finer than one operation.
