# Phone screens
## Why
On a phone the grid, pad, mixer overlay and menu all fought for one screen. The arranger and tracks table were buried in the menu or a dialog too wide to use.
## What changes
Below 760 px a bottom tab bar switches between four screens: Pattern (grid, pad, selection toolbar), Arrange (order chips, pattern settings, key), Mixer (the sidebar at full width) and Tracks (the tracks table). The controls are the same elements as on the desktop, moved into the screen while it is showing and returned afterwards, so nothing is duplicated.
## Capabilities
- **Modified:** `responsive-layout`
## Non-goals
Separate screens on wide displays; a Sounds screen (it stays a dialog).
