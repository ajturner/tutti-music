# Instruments made from sounds
## Why
One player was described in two places. A **track** in the song held its name, instrument, channel, columns, volume and pan, and was set up in Compose; its tuning, trim and release were **sound settings** kept in the browser, keyed by the instrument's id, and set up in Sounds. So tuning did not travel with the song, two tracks that used the fiddle were forced to share one tuning, and setting up a single player meant two panels. Compose had become a drawer for whatever was left over: the phrase's settings, the key and the tracks table.
## What changes
The song has **instruments**, each made from a **sound**. An instrument carries its name, sound, channel, columns, volume, pan, mute, solo, and now tune, cents, trim and release, all saved in the song (`song.instruments`, `instrument.sound`). Any number of instruments may use one sound; **⧉** duplicates one. A sound is what a bank provides: read-only, shared, with no settings of its own. One **Instruments** panel replaces Compose's tracks table and the Sounds panel: a row per instrument with details on demand, **+ instrument**, and underneath a **sound browser** with the banks, audition, add and the scopes. The settings of the song, a section or the open phrase (name, rows, row size, meter, groove, key) open in a sheet from a **▾** beside each crumb of the map. Preview on/off and samples or synth move to View. Panels are Files · Instruments · Connect · View. The per-browser `tutti.sounds.v1` settings are gone. Tuning, trim and release shape the preview only; exported MIDI keeps the written pitch. The Crossroads reel gains a second fiddle from the same sound. A new song starts with no instruments and opens on the Instruments panel; order and remove sit in an instrument's row when there is room.
## Capabilities
- **Modified:** `instruments-and-mixer` (was tracks-and-mixer), `sampled-orchestra`, `responsive-layout`, `song-model`
- **Renamed in place, wording only:** capability `instruments` is now `sounds`; "track" reads "instrument" in every spec except where MIDI file tracks are meant
## Non-goals
Renaming identifiers inside the code (`trackId`, `curTrack`, `INST`, `ev.track`): deferred to one mechanical commit after the direction is approved, so fixes to the format 4 branch keep merging cleanly. Renaming the `instruments` key inside bank.json files. Per-instrument keyswitch maps. Applying tune to MIDI out.
