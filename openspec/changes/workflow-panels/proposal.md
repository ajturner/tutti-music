# Workflow panels
## Why
The header had grown to nine captioned groups and about forty controls in one wrapping strip. The groups were named after implementation details (entry, columns, output) rather than what the composer is trying to do, file actions sat beside the song picker, the pattern settings were split across two groups, and the phone tab bar used a different model (Arrange, Mixer, Tracks) from the desktop header. Header height changed with window width, so nothing stayed where muscle memory expected it.
## What changes
The header keeps only identity and transport: the title, the song title, Play, Song, Stop, Rec, bpm, follow and the pattern selector, in two fixed rows. Everything else moves into panels named after workflows and opened from a menu bar: **Song** (open, new, delete, save, load, export), **Compose** (pattern settings, key, arrangement, tracks), **Sounds** (preview, sound, banks, instruments, scopes), **Connect** (MIDI, controller) and a small **View** menu (follow, pad, mixer, columns). One panel is open at a time; on a desktop it drops below the header as a sheet and the grid stays editable, on a phone it fills the screen and a tab bar (Pattern, Song, Compose, Sounds, Connect) switches between it and the grid. The Tracks dialog and the Arrange and Tracks phone screens are replaced by the Compose panel; the Sounds dialog becomes the Sounds panel. Octave and step leave the header and stay in the status line, which also gains step and opens Connect when its MIDI segment is clicked. A `?` button toggles the quick keys.
## Capabilities
- **Modified:** `responsive-layout` (panels replace the grouped header, folding menu and phone screens), `help` (? button), `pattern-grid` (status line shows step and opens Connect; columns live in View), `tracks-and-mixer` (tracks table in Compose; mixer toggled from View), `sampled-orchestra`, `audio-preview` (preview and sound in the Sounds panel), `midi-out` (Connect panel), `key-and-scale`, `groove` (Compose panel), `touch-input` (pad in View), `song-files` (Song panel), `note-entry` (step in the status)
## Non-goals
Render to audio, shareable links and a Launchpad page are planned entries in the Song and Connect panels but are not part of this change. The phone Mixer screen is dropped: the Compose panel's tracks table carries volume, pan, mute and solo, and the mixer overlay remains available from View.
